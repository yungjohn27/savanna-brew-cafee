About Savanna Brew Café

A welcoming place to pause

Savanna Brew Café was created for people who appreciate good food, good coffee and genuine hospitality. Located in Westlands, Nairobi, our café offers a comfortable place to meet, relax or get a little work done.

We believe that the best café experiences are built around fresh flavours, friendly service and a space where everyone feels welcome.
